History

This business was founded by Nhlamulo Gana in May 2026 to try to sell banana bread to students around his res.
he founded the business to raise money to ease the financial burden on his family and create income.
He learnt how to bake from his mother and used those skills to expand what he can do with an oven.

Our MISSION is to serve quality baked goods at a ridiculously low price, using fresh, carefully
sourced ingredients and honest baking practices passed down through generations, so that everyone can enjoy
a delicious treat without stretching their budget. This mission drives our VISION of becoming
a reliable, community-trusted supplier of good but affordable baked goods for every household, known not just
for our prices but for the warmth, consistency, and care we put into every single bake.


Nhlamulo Gana, the founder is your baker, customer service rep and your delivery guy.
