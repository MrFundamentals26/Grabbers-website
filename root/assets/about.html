KNOW MORE ABOUT US

OUR STORY

Grabbers was founded with a simple idea: nails are self-expression. What started as a small home studio grew into a full salon after word spread about the detail and creativity behind every set. Today, our team of nail artists combines technical precision with an eye for design trends, from timeless classics to bold, statement-making art.

We built Grabbers around the belief that every client deserves an experience as polished as the results: welcoming, relaxed, and tailored to you. Whether you're here for a quick refresh or a full custom design, we treat every appointment like the finishing touch on your look.


MISSION

At Grabbers, our mission is to deliver exceptional nail care that blends precision, creativity, and comfort. We strive to give every client a personalized experience from classic manicures to bold custom designs using quality products and techniques that leave you looking and feeling your best.

VISION

Our vision is to be the go-to nail destination known for creativity, consistency, and care. We aim to set the standard for nail artistry in our community, growing a space where clients feel confident, valued, and excited to express themselves through their nails.
